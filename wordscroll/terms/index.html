1. What WordScroll is

WordScroll is a vocabulary app. It ships a fixed set of word cards inside the download and works without a network connection. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no server that stores your progress.

2. What Plus is

WordScroll Plus unlocks the paid features described on the purchase screen you came from: all of Practice, with the Reading Level Test and your reading level's full report; no daily cap on new words or saves, and the top daily pace; every word pack; and every example and synonym a card holds.

The free plan keeps working exactly as it does today, and Earshot, the daily spelling bee, stays free once a day. Your saved words are never locked behind Plus — including words you saved while subscribed, after a subscription ends.

3. Plans, prices and billing

Plus is sold as an auto-renewing subscription or as a one-time lifetime purchase.

Every price, plan length and renewal term is shown to you by the app store on the purchase screen before you confirm, in your own currency. WordScroll displays what the store reports and never quotes a price of its own.

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period unless cancelled, and the store charges the payment method on your store account. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period, unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before that point.

4. Introductory offers

Some plans carry an introductory offer — a trial period, or a reduced price for the first period. Whether you can receive one is decided by the app store, not by WordScroll, and the store allows one introductory offer per subscription group per account.

The app asks the store before it draws any offer, so if you have already used yours you will see the regular price and no offer wording. When a trial period is offered and you do not cancel before it ends, the plan converts and the store charges the price shown on the purchase screen. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited if you purchase a subscription during the trial.

5. Cancelling

You cancel in your app store account settings, not in WordScroll — the store owns the subscription, so it is the only place a cancellation takes effect. The app links to that screen from Settings and from its trial reminder.

Cancelling stops the next renewal; access continues until the end of the period you have already paid for. When it ends, the app returns to the free plan: the daily cap and the free pace come back; Practice, the premium packs and the extra examples and synonyms lock again; and Earshot stays free once a day. Your streak, your saved words and your progress are not touched.

6. Restoring a purchase

Purchases belong to your app store account, so a new device or a reinstall restores them. Tap Restore purchases on any purchase screen or in Settings. If the store finds nothing on that account, the app tells you so plainly and nothing changes.

7. Refunds

Payments are processed by the app store, so refunds are requested from the store and are decided under its policy. WordScroll cannot see your payment details and cannot issue a refund itself. Your purchase is also governed by the app store's own terms of sale.
